Working in Peosta

Peosta is one of the fastest-growing communities in the Tri-State, and we work both ends of the spectrum here: established farmsteads with century-old timber that needs management, and new subdivisions where homeowners are clearing lots or doing landscape tree work for new builds.

Common Peosta tree species: bur oak and white oak (the giants on old farm properties), silver maple, sugar maple, black walnut (lots of these in this part of the county — valuable timber but they get big), green ash (dying from EAB), and a lot of volunteer trees on fence lines from years of unmaintained pasture borders.
