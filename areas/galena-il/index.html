Working in Galena

Galena's terrain is unlike anything else in the Tri-State — steep bluffs, narrow streets in the historic district, and mature trees on properties that have been there since the 1800s. We work both the historic downtown homes and the resort properties in the surrounding hills. Crossing into Illinois doesn't change our pricing or our service area.

Common Galena tree species: white oak, red oak, sugar maple, basswood (lots of these in the bluff forests), American beech, hackberry, and shagbark hickory. The historic district has many trees that pre-date most of the homes — they're protected by city ordinance in some cases, so removal often requires city approval. We can help with the paperwork.

Galena Tree Service FAQ

Are you licensed to work in Illinois?

Yes — fully insured for cross-state work. Galena is part of our regular service area.

Do you handle Galena historic district trees?

Yes. We're familiar with the process for tree removal in protected zones and can help guide you through city approval if your tree is restricted.
