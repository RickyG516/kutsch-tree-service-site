Working in Asbury

Asbury sits just west of Dubuque off Highway 20 and is one of our most active service areas. The neighborhoods around Asbury Road, Saratoga, and Plaza Drive have a lot of mature trees from when the area was developed in the 70s and 80s — and a lot of those trees are now hitting end-of-life or showing storm damage from recent severe weather.

Common Asbury tree species: silver maple (everywhere, fast-growing, prone to splitting), red oak and white oak (the keepers when healthy), green ash (mostly dead or dying from EAB), American elm and Chinese elm, blue spruce (popular landscape choice, often outgrowing yards), and pin oak. The wind exposure on Asbury's west-facing yards means we see a lot of storm damage from straight-line winds and ice events.

Asbury Tree Service FAQ

Do you work in Asbury subdivisions with HOAs?

Yes — we handle HOA approval paperwork if needed (some require notification before tree removal). Just let us know upfront if your subdivision has rules.

Are you available for emergency tree removal in Asbury?

Yes, 24-hour emergency. We can usually be on-site within 2-3 hours during storm events given the proximity to our Durango base.

What about Asbury's silver maples?

Silver maples are fast-growing and prone to splitting at the crotch — common storm-damage tree in Asbury. We see a lot of partial-failure silver maples that need either heavy pruning or full removal. Both are routine for us.

Do you offer multi-tree pricing?

Yes. If you have multiple trees that need work on the same property, we discount because we're already there with crew and equipment. Tell us when we quote.
