Tree Removal Cost in Dubuque, IA

Real numbers from real Iowa tree removal jobs. No hidden charges, no bait-and-switch.

The Honest Answer: $500 to $2,500

That's the range most residential tree removals fall into in the Dubuque, IA area. But pricing varies a lot tree-to-tree. Here's what actually drives the number.

Typical price tiers in the Dubuque area:

Small trees (under 30 ft, simple access): $300-$700

Medium trees (30-60 ft, some rigging): $700-$1,500

Large trees (60+ ft, near house or lines): $1,500-$3,000

Massive oaks / crane access required: $2,500-$5,000+

Storm-damaged or partially-down trees: Variable — often higher due to additional risk and rigging

What Drives the Price

  • Tree size (biggest factor). Height and trunk diameter determine how long the job takes and how much equipment we need. A 50ft maple costs less than a 70ft oak.
  • Proximity to structures. Open yard? Sometimes a clean fell. Tree leaning over the house? Full rigging job, every limb lowered by rope. Adds hours and skill premium.
  • Power lines. Working near energized lines requires coordination with the utility and slows everything down. Bumps the price.
  • Access for equipment. Can the bucket truck drive up to the tree? Faster, cheaper. Have to walk gear through a 36-inch gate? More time, more cost.
  • Condition of the tree. Healthy trees are predictable to climb. Rotted, hollow, or dead trees are dangerous — we charge a risk premium because rigging matters more.
  • Stump grinding. Included or separate? $150-$400 per stump on top of removal cost.
  • Hauling vs. leaving wood. Default is we haul the wood. Want firewood? Free, just tell us. Want it stacked nicely on the side of the house? Some labor, some cost.
  • Time of year. Winter often cheaper (frozen ground, less yard damage, lower demand). Storm season can be tighter.

What's Always Included

  • Climbing or bucket truck work — whatever the job calls for
  • Rigging when limbs need to be lowered (no "oops" damage)
  • Chipping all brush and small limbs
  • Hauling away the wood (unless you want to keep it)
  • Full cleanup — yard raked, work zone blown out, no leftover sawdust pile
  • Insurance coverage on every job (we can show proof)
  • Free on-site estimate — no charge, no pressure

Watch Out For: Pricing Red Flags

Some companies in the Tri-State area pull these moves. We don't.

  • "Phone quotes" without seeing the tree. Anyone giving you a firm number over the phone hasn't seen the access or the rigging needed. Final bill almost always goes up.
  • Knock-on-the-door pitches after a storm. Out-of-state crews working storm chases. Often unlicensed in Iowa. Often gone before any complaint can be filed.
  • Cash-only no-paperwork jobs. No invoice, no insurance proof, no recourse if something gets damaged.
  • "Topping" pitched as pruning. Cheaper because it's faster. Kills the tree in 3-5 years and weakens regrowth. ISA-trained pros don't top.
  • Vague "will be cleaned up" language. Get specifics. We tell you exactly what gets hauled, what stays, what the yard looks like when we leave.

Pricing FAQ

How much does tree removal cost in Dubuque, IA?

Most residential tree removals cost $500 to $2,500. Small jobs start around $300. Big oaks near houses can hit $3,000-$5,000+ when rigging or crane access is needed.

What makes tree removal more expensive?

Tree size (biggest factor), distance from structures or power lines, tree condition (dead trees cost more to climb safely), equipment access, and whether stump grinding is included.

Is stump grinding included?

Not by default — stumps are priced separately ($150-$400 each). If you want it included, ask when we quote and we'll bundle it.

Are estimates free?

Always. We come look, walk you through what it takes, and give you a real number. No charge, no obligation, no pressure.

Do you offer payment plans?

Most jobs are one-time payments due on completion. For larger lot clearing or multi-tree work, we can sometimes structure milestone payments. Ask when we quote.

Will my homeowner's insurance pay for tree removal?

Only if the tree fell on a covered structure (house, garage, fence, vehicle). Trees that just fell in the yard usually aren't covered. Storm damage often is. We give itemized estimates that adjusters accept.
