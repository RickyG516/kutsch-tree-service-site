About Kutsch Tree Service

Family-owned. Dubuque tough. Trees handled right since day one.

Who We Are

Kutsch Tree Service is a family-owned tree care company based right here in the Dubuque area. We're not a franchise. We're not subcontracted out to whoever's available that week. When you hire us, you get our crew on your property. Same faces. Same work ethic. Every time.

We've spent years climbing trees, clearing lots, and pulling storm-damaged limbs out of yards across the Tri-State area. Whether it's a 90-foot oak threading the line between two power poles or a stubborn stump that's been sitting in the backyard for a decade — we've handled it before. That experience saves you time, money, and headaches.

What you get with us: straight answers, fair quotes, on-time crews, and a yard that looks better when we leave than it did when we showed up. That's the whole pitch.
